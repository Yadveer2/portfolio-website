PROJECT 02

Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

Analyzed 7,043 telecom customers to identify churn drivers, quantify revenue loss, and prioritize retention efforts.

  • Used SQL (CTEs, window functions) and Pandas; found that 28% of customers—primarily first-year, month-to-month subscribers—accounted for 55% of churn.
